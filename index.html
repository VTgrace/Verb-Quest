📜 HOW TO PLAY

  1. Fill the blank with the correct Spanish verb. Correct answers damage monsters; mistakes cost 8–16 HP.
  2. Pick Type Answer or Answer Choices beside the question. Set Easy (infinitive and meaning) or Context (sentence only), and light/dark mode in Settings.
  3. Earn coins by answering correctly. Spend them on 🧪 Heal Potion (+30 HP), 🪙 Lucky Coin (3× coin rewards for 3 answers), or ⚡ Combo Spark (+3 combo and 2× your next hit).
  4. Defeat the slime, goblin, bat, and final Verb Dragon to win. After a win or defeat, review every answer or filter to missed questions before starting a new quest.
  5. Enter attacks in typing mode; H shows a hint; Space pauses; R restarts. Relics can be clicked during combat.
